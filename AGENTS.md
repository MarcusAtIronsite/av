# AGENTS.md

This directory is a fork of [aviator-co/av](https://github.com/aviator-co/av). Changes made
here are modifications to that upstream project, not to a standalone codebase.

## Fork modifications

`FORK.md` is the catalog of every modification this fork carries on top of upstream `master`.
It lists each modification, its files, its purpose, whether it is safe to upstream, and what it
depends on.

- Read `FORK.md` before editing code that touches the fork's customizations: the stack-comment
  diff annotations, the per-category diff breakdown, or the fork-branding link.
- When you add a modification to this fork, add or update its entry in `FORK.md` in the same
  change.
- Do not upstream the entry marked local-only; it points at the fork's own branding and must
  stay on the fork.
